# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone.

## 中文

我们承诺为所有参与者提供一个友好、包容、尊重他人的交流环境。

请保持以下原则：

- 使用礼貌、专业的语言
- 尊重不同背景、经验和观点
- 聚焦问题本身，不进行人身攻击
- 提供建设性的反馈

## English

We expect all participants to communicate with respect and professionalism.

Please follow these principles:

- Use courteous and professional language
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- Focus on the issue, not the person
- Provide constructive feedback

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include, but are not limited to:

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks
- Threats or intimidation
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission

## Reporting

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, please report it through the project’s issue channels or private contact methods, if available.
